Good to know

The 2026 details

Device-accurate fallback

libmonet only applies the SPEC_2026 dynamic-color role spec to the CMF style. TonalSpot falls back to SPEC_2025 roles, while keeping the 2025 palette rules. That is exactly what the reference gist shows.

Tertiary is not +60°

The 2025/2026 TonalSpot tertiary uses a piecewise hue rotation. For the loaded seed it lands on 110.71°, which is why a green wallpaper produces yellow-green, not cyan.

Local by design

Images are decoded, sampled, and quantized in this tab. No uploads, no analytics, no server round-trip. Export as Kotlin, JSON, CSS, Android XML, or a Figma variables bundle.
